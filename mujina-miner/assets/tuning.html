SIMULATED DATA — not from hardware. The numbers below are a layout preview.

Temperature and PVT mV are on-die sensors. Freq is that die's PLL0. Err % is the speed-log fail rate (fail ÷ (pass + fail)); GH/s is the speed-log pass count × 0.256 / 1000. Offsets are added to every PLL domain. Absolute MHz is PLL0 only and is saved as an offset, so a later board clock change moves it unless you choose Keep. There is no per-chip voltage: all 12 dies share the series rail, and 3600 mV is rejected. Edits are locked while ChipTuner is judging a trial.
